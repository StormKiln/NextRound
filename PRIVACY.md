# NextRound Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy describes the macOS NextRound app distributed through the Mac App Store, TestFlight, and the public [StormKiln/NextRound repository](https://github.com/StormKiln/NextRound). NextRound is maintained by StormKiln.

## Your workouts stay on your Mac

NextRound works without an account. Exercise selections, custom exercise descriptions, targets, timer settings, and completion checkmarks are used on your device to run your workout. We do not upload this workout information to our servers, share it with advertising or analytics services, or synchronize it between devices.

When you choose **Save workout**, the app stores the template's name, exercises, order, descriptions, targets, and timing configuration locally on your Mac. App preferences, such as available equipment, close-button behavior and update settings, are also stored locally. When you choose **Save result** after a completed workout, the app stores its completion date, active duration, workout configuration, exercise names/descriptions/targets, any completion checkmarks, and manually recorded AMRAP movement counts and partial progress locally. Optional notes attached to saved results are stored locally and can be edited or cleared from the result detail. Notes are included in local history search and are not uploaded. These records remain independent of later changes to exercise definitions or templates. Multi-part training sessions also store independent block prescriptions, complete/partial/skipped outcomes, and optional manually entered repetitions, time, distance, calories and load. While a multi-part session is active or awaiting a result decision, a local recovery checkpoint retains its position and entered progress. After an interruption, you can recover paused, review partial work or discard it; time away is not counted. A checkpoint is removed after saving or discarding. Standalone active-workout progress and unsaved setup drafts are not durable recovery records.

NextRound does not access HealthKit, read health records, connect to fitness equipment, or automatically measure your activity. Repetition, distance, and calorie targets are values you enter or select, not measurements collected from a device.

Personal exercises are stored locally when you explicitly save them to your library, including their name, description, category, equipment, focus areas, supported targets, optional default target and source identity when copied. They are not uploaded or synchronized. Editing a personal exercise does not rewrite existing workout copies.

## Exercise usage and suggestions

The exercise picker derives usage counts and exercise suggestions locally from retained saved results. A linked bundled or personal exercise counts once per saved workout or parent training session, regardless of repeated rounds, blocks or entries; untouched and skipped blocks do not contribute. Deleting a result removes its contribution. Custom or older entries without catalog identity are not attributed by name. Equipment preferences also filter eligible exercise choices locally and do not alter saved workout records. These suggestions do not send workout data to any external service and do not use an AI service.

## Files you export

You can export workout/session prescriptions and collections as local JSON files. These sharing files exclude history, notes, performance records and preferences. A full backup includes those private records and preferences; backup files are not encrypted. CSV exports contain the selected result data, with private notes only when requested. NextRound writes files where you choose and does not upload them. A cloud-synced destination may upload files under that service’s settings. Restore replaces local data after confirmation and retains a local rollback copy of the previous dataset. Exported files and rollback copies remain until you remove them; deleting an in-app record does not erase existing copies.

## No advertising or developer-operated analytics

The app includes no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics SDKs. We do not sell your workout data or use it for advertising. There is no NextRound account database or server-side copy of your saved workout templates or history.

## Updates and third-party services

**Mac App Store and TestFlight copies:** Apple distributes and updates these versions. They do not include NextRound's GitHub updater. Apple may process installation, purchase, crash, diagnostic, or beta-testing information under its policies and your settings. Information Apple makes available to us, such as beta feedback or diagnostic reports, is used to test, troubleshoot, and improve the app. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**GitHub download copies:** If automatic update checks are enabled, the app contacts GitHub for release information. Manual update checks and downloads also contact GitHub. These requests disclose ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request headers, to GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. They do not upload your workout templates, history or exercise notes. You can disable automatic checking in **Settings → Updates**. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**External links:** Opening the privacy policy, release notes, or support page opens a website in your browser. That website may process network information and use cookies under its own policy. NextRound does not send your workout contents with these links.

## Support and information you choose to share

If you contact us, we receive the message and any contact details, screenshots, or diagnostics you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate problems, and improve NextRound. Do not include sensitive workout or personal information unless needed for your request.

[GitHub issues](https://github.com/StormKiln/NextRound/issues) are public. Information posted there can be read and copied by others. Removing an issue does not remove copies independently retained by third parties.

## Retention, deletion, and your choices

- Saved templates remain on your Mac until you delete them from **Home → Saved workouts** or remove the app's associated local data. Preferences remain in local app storage. Deleting the app bundle alone may leave local data behind.
- Archived personal exercises remain in local library storage and can be restored. This release does not offer permanent deletion of individual library records; removing the associated local app data removes the library. Existing workout copies are independent.
- Saved results remain on your Mac until deleted from **Workout history** or until you remove the associated local data. Discarding a completed result does not add it to history.
- You can change app preferences in Settings and stop any active workout. No account deletion is needed because NextRound does not create an account.
- We retain support correspondence and diagnostic information accessible to us only as needed to handle support, resolve issues, or meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of information we control through the contact channel below. Apple and GitHub control data retained by their services under their respective policies.
- Device or cloud backups are controlled by your macOS and backup settings. Removing local data does not necessarily remove existing backups.
- GitHub and sandboxed Apple installations may use different local storage locations. Switching distribution channels does not automatically migrate templates or history.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this document and its effective date if NextRound's data practices change. The current policy is available from **Settings → About → Privacy policy** and at this public repository URL.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the NextRound maintainers through the [project's support page](https://github.com/StormKiln/NextRound/issues). Do not post private information in a public issue; ask for a private contact route when your request needs one.
