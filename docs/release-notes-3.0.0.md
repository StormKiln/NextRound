# NextRound 3.0.0

## Train with Spotify
Turn on **Play and pause Spotify with workouts** in **Settings → General** to link workouts to the Spotify desktop app. Starting or resuming a workout plays Spotify; pausing, stopping or finishing pauses it. The current track's title, artist and album artwork appear in the workout. In a multi-part session, music keeps playing between blocks and pauses when the session ends.

The setting is off by default. macOS asks once for permission to control Spotify, and you can change that later in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation**. NextRound never opens Spotify for you and only pauses music that a workout started, so recovering a workout or turning the setting on mid-workout won't interrupt what you're already playing. If Spotify is closed, slow to respond or not allowed, your workout carries on and a short note explains why.

## Cues you can hear over music
Every timing sound has been redesigned to cut through music: brighter and roughly twice as loud, with no distortion. Each still has its own shape: a short tock for the warning countdown, a double beep to start work or a new minute, a falling tone for rest and a rising chime when the workout finishes.

## Know when a timed hold is done
Exercises with a seconds target, such as a 40-second plank in an EMOM, now play a new quick triple pulse when the time is up. It works in EMOM minutes and Interval work phases. Countdown, AMRAP, For Time and Ladder are paced by you, so they don't use it. Targets as long as the whole minute or work phase finish with the usual beep or rest cue.
