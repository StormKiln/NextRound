# NextRound 1.6.0 — Find your focus

- Browse exercises by focus area or exercise type. Choose legs, glutes, core/abs, arms, shoulders, chest/pecs, back, lats or whole body; match any selected area alongside your equipment and search.
- Twenty new no-equipment movements bring the library to 83, including reverse crunches, bicycle crunches, bridge variations, unbanded clamshells, bear crawls and step jacks. Dead bugs and inchworms remain available.
- Search recognizes common forms such as “deadbugs”, “dead bugs”, “bird dogs” and “inchworms”.
- Suggestions, focus selection, grouping, search and the temporary equipment override remain intact when you visit Equipment settings from the picker.
- Saved history outside the current filters is no longer described as globally missing history.
- Picker search and Cancel stay visible while its content scrolls, including at the minimum window size.

Focus tags are catalog browsing aids. Group counts can overlap, while suggestions keep one identity per movement. Saved workout and history snapshots remain unchanged. All data stays local; privacy and the separate GitHub/App Store update channels are unchanged.

macOS desktop release; GitHub downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized. The App Store build is delivered separately through TestFlight.
