# App Store / TestFlight 3.3.0

All your favorites in one place. Every starred exercise now appears in a Favorites group at the top of the exercise picker, no longer limited to three suggestions. The library grows to 495 exercises with 22 new plank, cardio and kettlebell movements, including plank knee taps, dead-stop push-ups, kettlebell woodchoppers, gladiator deadlifts and the iron trident.

Review notes: No account, paid content, public user-generated content or external service is required for core functionality. Data stays on the Mac. App Store/TestFlight builds update through Apple. Optional Spotify control is unchanged from 3.0.0 and off by default.

To test: in any workout setup, choose Add exercise, star four or more exercises, and check that they all appear in the Favorites group at the top of the list. Search "kettlebell gladiator" or "plank knee tap" to find new movements.
