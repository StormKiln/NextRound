# NextRound 3.1.0

## Warm up and cool down with every workout
Every workout setup now has **Add a warm-up** and **Add a cool-down**. Check one to choose its length with a slider: 1 to 15 minutes, starting at 5. NextRound previews one movement per minute; use **Reshuffle** for a different set. The total time shows how long the whole session takes. Your choices are remembered for next time and apply to every workout type.

When you start, the warm-up runs first, then your workout, then the cool-down. Each warm-up and cool-down minute is a 45-second hold, marked by the done cue, followed by 15 seconds to change position before the next beep. The screen shows the current and next movement and the time left. Use **Skip warm-up** to go straight to your workout, or **End cool-down** to finish early. Only the very end plays the completion sound, and Spotify keeps playing between parts if you use Spotify control.

Your workout's result works exactly as before, with the same history, comparisons and scoring. History also notes how long you warmed up and cooled down. Repeat runs the same warm-up and cool-down again.

## Thirty new warm-up and cool-down movements
The library grows from 443 to 473 exercises.
- **Warm-ups:** World's greatest stretch, walking knee hugs and quad pulls, standing torso rotations, scapular wall slides, high-knee marches, lateral lunge shifts, walking leg cradles, straight-leg kick walks, half-kneeling thoracic rotations, squat-to-stands, kneeling ankle rocks, standing side reaches, reverse lunges with an overhead reach and standing cat-camels.
- **Cool-downs:** Pigeon, legs up the wall, doorway chest and hamstring stretches, couch stretch, banana stretch, frog pose, sleeper stretch, kneeling wrist and toe stretches, corpse pose, seated neck stretch, wall lat stretch, supine knee drops and an easy-pose forward fold.

Existing mobility movements that fit are tagged too, giving 44 warm-up and 53 cool-down options. Filter the library and exercise picker by **Workout phase**, and tag your own exercises as warm-up or cool-down movements in the exercise form.
