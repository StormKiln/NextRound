# App Store copy — 2.10.0

## What's New
Keep your chosen movements and fill the rest of your workout. Choose a total exercise count, filter by equipment, focus area, category, intensity or favorites, then preview random additions. Reshuffle until you're ready, apply them, or Undo to restore your original list.

Available across all six workout types and session blocks. Includes clearer generation feedback, small-window layout improvements, and interval-target fixes.

## Review notes
No account, paid content or external service is required for core functionality. All exercise selection runs locally. Open a workout, add one or two exercises, select Fill remaining slots, choose a larger total and filters, Preview additions, optionally Reshuffle additions, then Add to workout. Undo fill restores the original list. Existing Create a workout continues to replace all exercises.
