# NextRound 1.2.0

## Workout history, on your Mac
- Save completed EMOM, Countdown and Intervals sessions to your local training log.
- Review the completion date, active workout duration, exercise descriptions, planned targets and countdown checkmarks.
- Repeat a past workout by loading its configuration into setup, or delete an individual result with confirmation.
- Saved results are snapshots: later changes to exercises or templates do not rewrite your history.
- Save retries do not create duplicate sessions. Unsaved results are protected when leaving, quitting or installing an update.

## Fixes
- Cancelling a reorder preserves target edits and other exercise-list changes made during the reorder.
- The saved-workout confirmation accurately reflects the current setup.
- Exercise loading and failures have clear feedback instead of appearing as an empty search.

## Storage and distribution
History remains on your Mac; nothing new is uploaded. Existing saved workouts and settings are preserved. Only completed sessions you choose to save enter history; cancelled sessions and crash recovery are not included. Planned reps, distance and calories are not measured results. Scheduled interval rest is part of active workout duration; pauses and lead-in are excluded.

GitHub downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized, with signed updater assets. TestFlight/App Store builds use Apple's update channel. The channels have separate local storage and do not automatically transfer data. macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon.
