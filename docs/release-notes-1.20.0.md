# NextRound 1.20.0

## Look before you load

Use **Preview** on a saved workout to inspect its timing, ordered movements, descriptions and targets without replacing your current setup. Previews support all six workout types, including complete Ladder progression, interval work/rest timing and optional checklists.

The preview reads the saved snapshot on your Mac. Close it to keep working, or choose **Load workout** and confirm replacement. If the saved workout changes while you review it, NextRound shows the updated details and asks you to review again before loading. Missing or unreadable data leaves your current setup intact.

Long exercise lists scroll while Close and Load remain available. Timing distinguishes fixed durations, caps and workouts with no time cap; targets are prescriptions rather than predicted completion times.

## Smoother saved-workout browsing

- Renaming a workout outside the current search explains why it disappeared and offers **Show renamed workout**.
- A favourite save finishing in the background respects keyboard focus when you have moved to search or filters.
- Clearing filters restores focus to search, or to the library heading when no filter controls remain. Revealing a copied or renamed workout follows the same policy.

All 143 bundled exercises and existing document formats remain unchanged. For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. GitHub copies use the signed updater; TestFlight/App Store copies remain Apple-managed.
