# App Store / TestFlight 2.12.0

Swap just one random workout suggestion while keeping your other movements and pinned targets. Generator dialogs now protect changed drafts consistently and give clearer feedback when no exercise matches your choices. Improved spacing and keyboard controls keep suggestion previews easy to use. Twenty new jump-rope, bar, slam-ball and dumbbell exercises bring the library to 443.

Review notes: No account, paid content, public user-generated content or external service is required for core functionality. Data stays on the Mac. App Store/TestFlight builds update through Apple. Test: open a workout, select Fill remaining slots, preview additions, pin one, Swap another, apply and Undo. Swaps affect only unpinned positions and exclude movements already in the preview; exhausted pools preserve the preview. Filter/count/library changes invalidate it.
