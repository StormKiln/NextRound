# App Store metadata — 2.1.0

## What’s New

Find a different movement with curated exercise alternatives. See why it is suggested, review equipment and targets, then confirm or cancel. Available in all six workout builders and session blocks, with Undo.

Explore 20 new movements, bringing the library to 243: equipment-free poses, push-up and lunge variations, dumbbell rotations, and kettlebell holds, carries and dead-stop swings.

Session improvements prevent hidden incompatible performance values, keep sessions accessible when collections fail to load, protect collection edits and handle cancelled exports quietly.

## Review notes

No login, sample files, payments, social posting, AI service or regional feature differences. From any workout builder, add a bundled movement and choose Replace. Curated choices show a Review alternative button; confirm with Use this alternative. Equipment filters still apply. Custom movements can use ordinary replacement. From Home, Build a session offers the same controls inside each block. Actual performance entry is manual and distinct from targets. Use a physical-device recording and screenshots of the submitted build for App Review; browser captures do not substitute for Apple's requested recording.
