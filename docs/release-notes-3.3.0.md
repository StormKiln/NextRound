# NextRound 3.3.0

## All your favorites in one place
When you add an exercise, a **Favorites** group now sits at the top of the list with every exercise you've starred. It's open by default, sorted alphabetically, and follows your search, equipment, phase and focus filters. Before, the picker's **My favorites** button showed only three starred exercises. That button is gone, and Most used, Try something new and Mix it up still give suggestions from your history. Starring or unstarring an exercise anywhere in the picker updates the group straight away. **Create a workout** and **Fill remaining slots** still let you build from your favorites.

## 22 new movements
The library grows from 473 to **495 exercises**.
- **Bodyweight:** plank knee tap, plank rotation, plank with alternating arm and leg lift, squat cross-knee crunch and dead-stop push-up.
- **Kettlebell:** curl, sumo squat, woodchopper, ballistic row, curl to pullover, crush-grip overhead press, squat to calf raise, sit-up to stand, overhead triceps extension, floor pullover, overhead march, iron trident, kneeling swing press, deep squat with curl and press, goblet reverse lunge, gladiator deadlift and split-stance row.

Search also finds more familiar names: *squat jumps*, *plank jacks*, *buttkickers*, *kettlebell gorilla rows* and *kettlebell halos* now find the existing movements.
