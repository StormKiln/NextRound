# App Store metadata — 2.0.0

## What’s New

Build complete training sessions from warm-up to cooldown using all six workout modes. Add recovery between blocks, choose automatic or Ready transitions, and save or share the whole routine.

Record optional actual performance separately from targets, review one session with per-block results, and compare matching attempts. Recover interrupted sessions paused, without counting time away.

Protect your local training data with full backup, previewed restore and a rollback copy. This release also improves validation, small-window controls and unsaved-edit protection.

## Description additions

BUILD YOUR WHOLE SESSION
Combine EMOM, Countdown, Intervals, AMRAP, For Time and Ladder into a routine of up to 20 blocks. Warm up, train, recover and cool down. Choose automatic transitions or start the next block when you are ready.

RECORD WHAT YOU DID
Enter optional actual reps, seconds, metres, calories and load in kg or lb. Planned targets stay separate. Review complete, partial and skipped blocks in one saved session and compare compatible recorded attempts.

KEEP YOUR TRAINING SAFE
Recover an interrupted session paused at its last saved position. Export a full local backup and preview a restore before replacing your data. No account required; your training stays on your Mac.

## Review notes

The app is offline and requires no login or sample files. From Home choose Build a session, add workout blocks, and start; or use any of the six standalone workout cards. Session results are explicitly saved. Actual performance entry is manual and optional, not sensor measurement. Settings → Backup exports or restores local data after confirmation. No social posting, payments, authentication, AI services or regional feature differences have been introduced. Use the physical-device recording and screenshots of the submitted build when submitting App Review; automated browser screenshots do not substitute for Apple's requested recording.
