# NextRound 1.7.0 — Finish on your terms

- **For Time workouts:** count elapsed workout time, optionally set a cap, and follow an ordered exercise checklist. Finish when you are done; the confirmation pauses the timer. Lead-in and pauses do not count toward your result.
- **Clear outcomes:** manual finishes and reached time caps stay distinct in saved history. Completing a checklist never ends the timer automatically. Save a template, repeat a workout or review its targets and checked movements.
- **20 new exercises:** ten core and ten whole-body movements bring the library to 103, including hollow-body rocks, bear planks, skater bounds, Turkish get-ups and dumbbell combinations. Fifteen need no equipment; loaded movements state equipment requirements. All targets are editable.
- Searching again reopens matching groups, while your manual collapse remains respected until the query changes.
- Empty exercise results explain search, focus and equipment constraints with nearby recovery buttons. Temporary equipment overrides do not change saved preferences.
- Wider dialogs, wrapping actions and reserved scrollbar space keep controls accessible at small window sizes.
- Setup and history screens load when opened from bundled local files. Failed screen loads offer retry with draft preservation; active timers remain immediately available.

Existing workout templates and history remain local and compatible. The GitHub and App Store builds retain their separate update channels. GitHub downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized; the App Store build is delivered separately through TestFlight.
