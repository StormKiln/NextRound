# NextRound 1.10.0 — Compare your repeat attempts

- Open a saved result in **Workout history** and choose **Compare attempts** to review sessions with matching saved movements, targets and workout rules. Finished For Time and Ladder attempts compare active time; AMRAP compares recorded progress. Time-cap outcomes remain separate, and fixed-duration workouts do not claim performance gains from a scheduled clock.
- History search now recognizes the same movement spelling and spacing aliases as the exercise picker, while retaining searches of your saved descriptions.
- Generated Intervals workouts keep automatically suggested time targets within the configured work phase. Explicit personal targets remain unchanged and show a warning when they exceed that phase.
- Corrected singular/plural wording in workout setup, exercise usage, saved workouts, history and completion views.

Comparisons are calculated locally from saved snapshots. Changes to the current exercise library or templates do not rewrite previous results. Legacy movements without library identity remain separate from attributed movements; repeating a saved workout preserves its snapshot for matching. Targets and checkmarks are planned or self-reported work, not measured performance.

No account or migration is required. GitHub downloads update through the signed updater; Mac App Store and TestFlight copies update through Apple.
