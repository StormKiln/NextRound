# NextRound 1.9.2 — Build your next workout

- Added **Create a workout** to all six workout setup pages. Choose **My Favorites**, **Try something new**, or **Mix It Up**, then choose how many exercises to generate. Your timing stays unchanged, targets remain editable, and **Undo generated workout** restores the previous list until you edit it.
- Generated workouts use saved exercise history and your equipment preferences. If too few eligible movements are available, NextRound explains how to adjust the count or equipment settings.
- Fixed spacing between **My exercises** and the workout cards on Home.
- **Show all equipment** now reflects the actual picker filter. With no saved equipment, it starts checked; uncheck it to browse equipment-free movements. Temporary choices do not overwrite saved equipment preferences.

Saved workouts, personal exercises and workout history remain on your Mac. Mac App Store and TestFlight copies update through Apple; GitHub downloads use the signed updater.
