# NextRound 2.11.0

## Keep the suggestions you like
In **Fill remaining slots**, preview additions and pin any movement you want to keep. Reshuffle draws only the unpinned slots while preserving pinned positions and targets. Apply adds the reviewed list; Undo restores your original exercises. Works across all six workout types and session blocks.

Pins are temporary to the current preview, separate from exercise favorites. Changing filters, the total or available library invalidates the preview and pins. When every addition is pinned, unpin one to reshuffle. Random draws can repeat unpinned movements.

## More dependable generation
- Retry now reloads session history when a previous read failed.
- Create a workout refuses to overwrite exercises changed while its dialog is open.
- Exercise count receives initial focus in both generation dialogs.
- Clear filters resets Fill filters to your saved equipment preference without changing the requested total.
- Preview rows wrap long names and separate movement targets from Pin controls; actions remain visible at smaller window sizes.

## Twenty new movements
The bundled library grows from 403 to 423 exercises, with new no-equipment warm-up, core, lower-body and conditioning options: arm circles, arm swings, heel beats, reverse clamshell, curtsy lunge, ankle hops, quick feet, standing side and kick crunches, cross-body mountain climbers, forearm-plank leg lifts, prone back extensions, lying side and oblique V crunches, open-leg rocker, plank kick-through, Hindu push-up, diamond kicks, bear squat and squat to side kick.

Each has technique guidance, supported targets and equipment/focus/intensity metadata. Starting targets are editable suggestions; review the workout before starting. Existing exercises, saved workouts and results remain unchanged.
