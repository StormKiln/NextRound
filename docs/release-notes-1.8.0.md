# NextRound 1.8.0 — One rung at a time

- **Ladder workouts:** the sixth workout mode follows ascending, descending or pyramid rep targets. Preview the full progression and planned total, choose rep-compatible exercises or Custom movements, and optionally set a time cap.
- Mark each movement complete at your own pace, with Undo for corrections. Completing the last movement pauses the timer for confirmation. Lead-in and pauses never count toward active workout time.
- Ladder results distinguish finished workouts from reached caps and preserve the movements you marked complete, the rep sequence and the next planned target. Repeat opens an editable setup. Targets and marked progress are not automatically measured performance.
- **Update saved workouts:** load a template, edit it, then update it in place or save a new copy. If the original changed or was deleted, your edits stay available without overwriting the other change.
- **Find prior sessions:** combine workout-type filters with exercise-name/description search in history. Detail views and deletion keep the selected session identity.
- Setup errors stay with the relevant workout mode. Correcting one field preserves other errors, and invalid Start/Save actions focus the first relevant control with linked explanations.

Ladder uses one rep sequence for every selected movement. Starting reps and increment are 1–1000; 1–50 rungs (up to 99 for a pyramid); 1–100 movements; optional cap 1 second to 24 hours. Descending targets must remain positive. No automatic conversion of time, distance or calories to reps.

All 103 existing exercises and saved snapshots remain unchanged. Data stays local. GitHub downloads and App Store/TestFlight builds retain their separate update channels; no new service or dependency is introduced. See [exercise taxonomy and content policy](exercise-taxonomy.md).
