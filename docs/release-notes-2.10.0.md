# NextRound 2.10.0

## Fill the rest of your workout
Choose the movements you want, then use **Fill remaining slots** to randomly add the rest. Pick a total exercise count and narrow additions by equipment, focus area, category, intensity or starred favorites.

Preview additions, reshuffle them, and apply when ready. Your chosen movements, targets and order stay intact, as does workout timing. Undo restores the original list. Duplicate movements are excluded by identity and normalized name, including custom names. If too few movements match, the dialog explains how to adjust your total or filters.

Works across EMOM, Countdown, Intervals, AMRAP, For Time and Ladder, including session block editing. Ladder suggestions support repetitions. Existing Create a workout presets still replace the full list.

## Reliability and usability
- Changed filters, library data or workout configuration cannot apply an outdated preview.
- Load errors offer Retry while preserving your workout and stored data.
- New generated personal seconds targets fit an interval's work phase; retained targets are unchanged.
- Undo is cleared after a later exercise-list edit.
- Scrollable preview with separated controls and fixed actions for smaller windows.

The exercise library remains at 403 movements. Random suggestions are a starting point: review movements and targets before starting. Reshuffling may repeat a previous random result.
