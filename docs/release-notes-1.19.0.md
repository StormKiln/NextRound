# NextRound 1.19.0

## Generate for your focus

Choose focus areas directly in **Create a workout**, alongside My favorites, Most used, Try something new or Mix It Up. Select one or more areas, or leave All areas selected. Movements match any selected area; Back includes Lats, and Whole body is its own category.

Focus choices combine with available equipment and workout compatibility across all six modes. The eligible count updates as you choose. Your exercise count, workout timing and Undo remain under your control. If too few movements match, adjust the count or filters explicitly. Suggestions do not guarantee coverage of every selected area or a balanced training plan.

Focus selections stay with the open generation flow, including visits to equipment settings. A new generation flow starts with All areas. The dialog keeps Generate and Cancel available while its contents scroll.

## Easier library recovery

- Keyboard focus returns to a useful control after archiving a personal exercise or clearing an empty bundled search.
- Invalid personal-exercise defaults and missing supported units focus the field that needs attention and provide associated errors.
- Changing picker filters reveals the beginning of results instead of retaining an old scroll position.

All 143 existing movements and stored workouts remain unchanged. For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. GitHub installations use the signed updater; Apple installations remain managed by TestFlight/App Store.
